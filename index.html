Overview

The purpose of this website is to act as a personal wildlife photoblog to sort my photos by location and talk about some of my experiences with phoographing the animals in these places. My goal is for this website to be something I can share with family and firends to remind us of these trips, but also to show people interested in wildlife photography what some of these places have to offer and share just a little bit fo experience with finding them. I have also included bits on conservation for the species depicted in my galleries since quite a few do face some serious threats that I would like visitors to be aware of, and I have even included a couple of personal tips for approaching certain species with this in mind. Please enjoy checking out the galleries linked above!
